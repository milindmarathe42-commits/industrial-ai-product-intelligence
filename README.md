# 🤖 Industrial AI Product Intelligence

### AI-Powered Manufacturing Quality Inspection Platform

> **Built by Millu's Coders**

Industrial AI Product Intelligence is an AI-powered product inspection platform designed to automate manufacturing quality inspection using computer vision and generative AI.

The platform allows users to upload product images, detect products using YOLOv8, generate intelligent inspection reports using Gemini AI, visualize quality analytics, compare original and AI-detected images, and download professional PDF inspection reports.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional manufacturing quality inspection often depends heavily on manual inspection.

This can lead to:

- ⏱️ Time-consuming inspection processes
- 👨‍🔧 Human dependency
- ⚠️ Inconsistent quality assessment
- 📋 Manual report generation
- 📊 Limited production analytics
- 🔍 Difficulty tracking inspection history

Industrial AI addresses these problems by combining computer vision, artificial intelligence, and automated reporting into a single platform.
